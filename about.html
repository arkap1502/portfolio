The longer story

I learn by building.

My cybersecurity journey started with curiosity — how systems are protected, how attacks happen and how technology can detect them. At GNIT I built foundations in Python, JavaScript, SQL, Flask, Linux and Git, then earned Cisco's cybersecurity and hardware certificates. Password security and phishing detection became my first builds, growing into web security, Website Security Copilot and an always-on Laptop Antivirus.

Deloitte and Commonwealth Bank simulations drilled incident response, penetration testing and security awareness. Datacom's AI program showed me AI-assisted defence and debugging, and a verified internship at Thiranex added guided, hands-on experience. I learn by building rather than only studying — and I am still building.
