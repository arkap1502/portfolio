URL Scanner

A cybersecurity-focused URL scanner that turns weak signals into a clear verdict. It flags raw IPs, uncommon TLDs, lookalike brand spellings, phishing keywords and missing security headers, then combines them into a 0–100 risk score with a plain-language explanation.

  • URL & typosquat detection
  • Security header analysis
  • HTTPS & directory checks
  • Suspicious keyword & TLD flags
  • 0–100 risk score
  • Plain-language security report
