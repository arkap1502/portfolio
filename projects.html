FEATURED Cybersecurity · Browser Extension

Website Security Copilot

AI-powered assistant that scans, explains, and helps fix website security issues — plus an Always-On browser guard.

  • It is a browser add-on — the live demo shows how it looks, full protection starts after you add it to your browser
  • One-click website check — it checks safety settings, site lock, cookies, open doors and email safety
  • Simple ON/OFF guard — when ON, it watches every page you open, marks it Safe, Doubtful or Blocked, and blocks bad sites by itself
  • For each problem, it shows how serious it is, gives proof, explains in easy words, and gives a ready fix plus A to F grade
  • Fully safe to use — it only looks, never harms any site, follows site rules, and the AI only explains what it found
  • Gives a simple 0 to 100 safety score with clear fixes anyone can follow
Next.jsTailwind CSSFastAPIPythondnspythonSQLiteDockerMV3 Extension

FastAPI scan engine (httpx, ssl, dnspython) checks security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options), cert expiry, cookie flags, open ports / fingerprinting, DNS-email (SPF, DMARC, DKIM) & phishing signals; MV3 extension (webNavigation + tabs) runs passive verdicts in background. LLM only explains tool output — risk score 100 - min(100, 10*C + 5*H + 2*M + 1*L), grades A–F. Stack: Next.js + Tailwind, FastAPI, Python, SQLite → Postgres, Docker, MV3 extension.

FEATURED Cybersecurity · Password Security

Altron Password Inspector

A simple tool that makes strong passwords and tells you how safe they are.

  • Makes truly random strong passwords that hackers cannot guess
  • You can choose length, letters, numbers and symbols, plus show, copy and clear buttons
  • Shows live password power — weak, medium or strong — right away
  • Works fully in your browser — your passwords never go anywhere
JavaScriptHTMLCSSWeb Crypto API

Passwords are generated using crypto.getRandomValues() rather than Math.random(), since the former is cryptographically secure and unsuitable-for-security pitfalls of pseudo-random generators are avoided. Strength scoring checks for lowercase, uppercase, digit, and punctuation character classes plus a 15+ character length threshold.

Web Security · Phishing Detection

URL Threat Scanner

Paste any link and it tells you if it is safe or a fake trap — with a 0 to 100 score.

LOW RISKMEDIUM RISKHIGH RISK
  • Gives a simple 0 to 100 danger score with a clear safe-or-not answer
  • Catches fake links — wrong numbers in place of names and short hidden links
  • Catches copy-cat brand names and common fraud words
  • Shows a colour bar for risk and works fully in your browser
JavaScriptHTMLCSS

Entirely client-side pattern analysis: the scanner inspects the URL string for structural red flags (raw IPs in place of a domain, uncommon TLDs, lookalike brand spelling, and common phishing keywords) and combines them into a 0–100 score with a plain-language verdict.

FEATURED Cybersecurity · Prompt Injection Detection

Hidden-Prompt Scanner for Documents

Finds hidden cheat messages in files that try to fool AI — all checked safely on your side.

  • Finds hidden text — white text on white page, too-small or see-through text, text outside the page, and secret blank letters
  • Checks file details, notes and comments for trick lines like “forget old orders”
  • Gives Low, Medium or High danger level with a clear report on web and computer
  • Everything is checked privately — nothing is sent outside
PythonFlaskPyPDF2python-docxBeautifulSoupRender

Extracts visible text plus layout signals (color, font size, opacity, position) to flag color ≈ background, size ≤ 1.5pt, opacity == 0 and off-page content, including zero-width chars (U+200B/C/D, U+FEFF); checks PDF metadata, DOCX properties, comments & footnotes, then regex-matches injection phrases like ignore previous instructions into a combined LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH risk score — fully local via scanner.py CLI and Flask web UI. Stack: Python, Flask, PyPDF2, python-docx, BeautifulSoup — deployed on Render.
